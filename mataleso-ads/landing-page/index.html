The room is simple. The coffee isn't. We decided early on which one mattered more.

Coffee people come back for

  • Si Hitam Cold Brew

    Si Hitam was brewed last night. It's been waiting quietly. That's kind of the whole point of it.

  • Balinese Tangerine

    We've watched enough people take their first sip of the Balinese Tangerine to know what the face looks like. It's a good face.

  • Kopi Susu Mataleso Nusantara

    We named it Nusantara for a reason. It's not just a kopi susu. You'll understand that after the first sip more than we could explain it now.

  • Pistachio Matcha

    It started as something we were curious about. It's now the drink people describe to their friends before they even arrive.

  • Temple of Rose

    We've seen people order Temple of Rose and then sit very still for a while. We think that's the right response.

  • Mango Kopyor

    Makes complete sense if you grew up with it. Requires a small leap of faith if you didn't. Either way, people finish it.

  • Klepon

    Some people come in for the coffee and leave having also eaten two klepon. We don't question it.

Food, the way it's made at home

Traditional Indonesian dishes from across the islands, cooked fresh when you order.

  • Se'i

    Smoked meat from our family farm in Flores. It's the thing we're proudest of, so we'll keep this short.

  • Nasi goreng se'i

    The same se'i, in a plate of nasi goreng.

  • Beef rendang, soto betawi, siomay, sup brenebon

    From Padang, Jakarta, Bandung and Manado. One kitchen, a lot of islands.

  • Klepon and dadar gulung

    Made with no artificial colouring.

Why Mataleso

Mataleso is the Manggarai word for sun. We open with it and stay open long after it's gone. In between, there's coffee, Indonesian pastry, and music we take more seriously than most people would think is reasonable.
